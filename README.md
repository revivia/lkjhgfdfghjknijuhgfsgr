# arDJuino!

Meet arDJuino!
Have you ever wanted to remix your favorite song or create your own DJ set? 

arDJuino is a mini DJ controller, controlled by an Arduino R3. It allows you to control functions such as synth, volume, and pause/play. These functions are controlled by a mini joystick, ultrasonic sensor, potentiometer and button. Arduino IDE, Hairless Midi, LoopMidi, and FL Studio are required to run and operate the project. By moving your hand closer and farther away from the sensor, the synth fluctuates. The potentiometer acts as a knob to control the volume and the button is used for pause/play. The joystick is free to be linked to any function on FL studio, depending on the preferences of the user.
